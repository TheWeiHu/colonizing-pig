    How To Play

    Two players race to 15 victory points. Starting order is randomly assigned. Place two starting settlements, each followed by a road. The player who places second gets both middle placements. You receive resources beside your second settlement.

    Roll the dice to produce resources. Settlements earn one card from neighboring matching tiles; cities earn two. Trade with the bank, build roads and settlements, upgrade cities, or buy development cards. The robber blocks its tile.

    • Road: 1 lumber + 1 brick.
    • Settlement: 1 lumber + 1 brick + 1 wool + 1 grain. Worth 1 point.
    • City: 2 grain + 3 ore. Worth 2 points.
    • Development card: 1 wool + 1 grain + 1 ore. Newly bought cards become playable next turn.

    Longest Road and Largest Army are worth 2 points each. On a seven, a hand above 9 cards discards half, rounded down. The friendly robber protects a player below 3 public points. Balanced dice and bank/port trades follow the two-player engine rules; there is no player-to-player trading.

    Resource hands are trackable in two-player play and shown in Game Details. The bot's development cards remain hidden. For small screens, Zoom Board enlarges the targets; scroll to reach the edges.
